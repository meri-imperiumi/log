We are standing at the edge of the biggest sea voyage we'll ever take on. The Pacific, the biggest stretch of open water in the world. A sea so big that the idea of sailing across it doesn't fully fit my brain. At any given moment at least one aspect of it slips over the horizon beyond comprehension. So there is nothing more to it than to sail, to experience what we cannot imagine: to head into the unknown of the voyage driven by wanderlust, a compulsion to reach further and further beyond our wildest dreams.
